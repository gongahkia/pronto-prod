# Client Services Agreement Draft

Working draft for Singapore pilots. This is not legal advice and should be reviewed by counsel before use.

## Parties

- Service provider: Pronto.
- Client: the business receiving AI customer-service operations support.

## Scope Of Work

Pronto will configure and operate a purpose-driven customer-service assistant for agreed channels, initial knowledge sources, guardrails, escalation rules, and operator dashboard access.

The assistant may answer customer-service questions from approved client sources and may escalate when a human owner should respond.

The assistant must not process payments, issue refunds, cancel or modify bookings, change orders, give legal or medical advice, or act as a general-purpose AI assistant.

## Client Responsibilities

- Provide accurate knowledge sources, policies, brand voice guidance, escalation contacts, and channel credentials.
- Review and approve launch scope before live traffic.
- Keep Pronto informed of policy, pricing, menu, availability, or operational changes.
- Respond to escalations within the agreed SLA.

## Fees

Pilot pricing should be set in the order form. Benchmark placeholders:

- Setup fee: SGD 2,500.
- Monthly retainer: SGD 1,200.
- Included conversations: 500 per month.
- Additional channels, integrations, analytics, and multilingual support may be priced separately.

## Service Levels

Pilot SLA should distinguish:

- AI response target for in-scope sourced questions.
- Human owner escalation response target.
- Channel/provider outage handling.
- Planned maintenance notice.

## Data Protection

Pronto will process client customer data only to provide the service, maintain security, perform support, and meet legal obligations. A separate DPA should govern personal-data processing, retention, breach notification, deletion, subprocessors, and offboarding.

## IP And Materials

The client owns its source materials, policies, brand materials, and customer data. Pronto owns its platform, templates, code, workflows, prompts, dashboards, and general know-how, excluding client confidential information.

## AI Output And Liability

AI outputs may be incomplete or incorrect. Pronto will use agreed guardrails, retrieval, review, and escalation workflows to reduce risk. The client remains responsible for final business decisions, refunds, bookings, legal positions, and customer commitments made by humans.

## Confidentiality

Each party must protect non-public business, technical, pricing, customer, and credential information disclosed by the other party.

## Termination And Offboarding

Either party may terminate according to the order form. On termination, Pronto should export client data, disable integrations, delete credentials, and delete client customer data within the agreed retention window.

## Liability Cap

Set a liability cap in the order form. A common pilot placeholder is fees paid in the previous three months, excluding liability that cannot be limited under applicable law.

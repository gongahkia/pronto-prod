# DPO Record And PDPC Notification Checklist

Working draft for Singapore pilots. This is not legal advice.

## Internal DPO Record

- DPO name: Keith Tang, unless the founders revise responsibility split.
- Backup contact: Astin or Gabriel, to be confirmed internally.
- Public contact email: to be created before production.
- Responsibilities:
  - Maintain data protection policies.
  - Triage personal-data incidents.
  - Coordinate client and PDPC notifications if required.
  - Maintain data retention and offboarding records.
  - Review subprocessors and credential handling.

## PDPC-Facing Checklist

- Confirm the formal DPO appointment internally.
- Publish business contact information for data protection queries.
- Keep an internal record of the DPO appointment and backup coverage.
- Prepare breach assessment and notification workflow.
- Review PDPC guidance before submitting any notification.
